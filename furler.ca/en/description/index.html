What you should know about the Simplicity sail furler

Set it and forget it!

Simplicity is a high-quality reefing furler, designed for a long life on your sailboat. It is made from white baked painted aluminium, stainless steel rigging and nylatron bushings — a mix of nylon for a smooth surface finish, teflon for durability and graphite for permanent self-lubrication.

An important safety device for the single-handed sailor

  • No forestay modification required.
  • No bearings, no springs.
  • It has its own halyard.
  • You buy direct from the manufacturer.
  • Virtually unseizable.
  • Sold complete, with deck pulley and cleat.

Invented and built in Canada. Already 1,500 units sold across the country.
